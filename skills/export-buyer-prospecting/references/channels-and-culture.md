# Channel and culture by market

Read this before the first touch in a new country, or when email gets no reply.

## Channel order (first touch → follow-up)
| Market | First touch | Then | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Germany, Austria, Switzerland | LinkedIn note or strictly individual email | Email, phone | Consent rules for email ads (UWG §7); expect data, certificates, test reports |
| UK, Netherlands, Nordics | Email | LinkedIn | Direct, brief; English fine |
| France, Italy, Spain | Email in their language | LinkedIn, phone | Local-language emails get far more replies than English |
| USA, Canada | Email | LinkedIn, phone | Short, ROI and reliability first; CAN-SPAM/CASL footer |
| Mexico, Latin America | Email in Spanish/Portuguese | WhatsApp after they reply | WhatsApp is the main business channel once contact exists |
| Middle East (UAE, Saudi) | Email or LinkedIn | WhatsApp, phone, visit | Relationship and respect before price; avoid Friday (weekend) |
| Africa (Nigeria, Kenya, South Africa) | Email | WhatsApp | Screen for advance-fee and fake-buyer scams (see Gotchas) |
| India | Email | WhatsApp, phone | Highly price-driven; quick replies expected |
| Japan, Korea | Email in local language, formal | Distributor intro, trade show | Slow, consensus decisions; precision and quality evidence |
| Southeast Asia | Email | WhatsApp / LINE / Zalo, phone | Many buyers prefer chat or calls to email |

## Rules for social channels
- **LinkedIn**: complete the profile first (photo, headline "Helping EU importers source X"), connect with a short note about *them*, interact with a post, then message. Do not paste the cold email into the note.
- **WhatsApp**: only after the buyer gave the number or wrote first. Business policy requires opt-in and stopping on request; blocks and reports throttle your number. Max 3 nudges in a short window, then switch to slow nurture. A reply from the buyer opens a 24-hour window for free-form replies on the Business Platform.
- **Phone**: works well for SMB owners in US, LATAM, India and SEA. Call in their morning, 30-second opener, offer to email a one-pager.

## Culture notes that change the email
- Germany: numbers, certifications (LFGB, CE, REACH), test reports. "Quality is good" says nothing.
- USA: what it saves or earns them, lead time, US warehouse or DDP option.
- Middle East: courtesy, relationship, flexibility on terms; avoid hard push.
- Japan: formal tone, apologise for the unsolicited contact, precise specs.
- Latin America: warmth, local language, payment terms matter.
