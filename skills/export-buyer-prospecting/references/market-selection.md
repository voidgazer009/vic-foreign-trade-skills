# Choosing target markets (step 0)

Read this when the user does not know which countries to target.

1. Get the HS code (6 digits) of the product.
2. **ITC Trade Map** (trademap.org, free registration): Product → "List of importing markets" for the HS code. Note the top 15 importers, 5-year growth, and China's share in each.
3. **UN Comtrade** (comtradeplus.un.org) for detail, **ImportYeti** (free, US sea shipments) to see named US importers and their current suppliers.
4. Score each candidate country 1–5 on: import value, growth, China's share (high = buyers already source from China), tariff/anti-dumping on Chinese origin (check the market's customs tariff or WTO tariff database), certification burden, and the user's existing customers nearby.
5. Pick 2–3 markets, not a continent. Narrow focus is what makes the first email personal.

Output a short table: Country · Imports (USD) · 5-yr growth · China share · Barriers · Score · Why.
