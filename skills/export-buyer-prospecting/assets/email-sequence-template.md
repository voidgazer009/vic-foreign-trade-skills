# Cold outreach sequence (4 touches, about 14 days)

Placeholders in {braces}. Write in English unless the buyer's site is only in their own language; then write in that language. Each email under 120 words, plain text, no attachments.

## Email 1 · Day 0 · The specific reason
Subject: {Product} for {their line / season / catalogue}

Hi {FirstName},

{One sentence on the specific fact you found about them.} We make {product, one concrete spec} in {city} for {country/region} {buyer type}s, {one proof the user confirmed: certification / MOQ / lead time}.

{One question that is easy to say yes to, e.g. "Would a sample of our two best sellers for {country} be useful?"}

{Name}, {Title}, {Company}, {Postal address}
Reply "no thanks" and I won't write again.

## Email 2 · Day 3 · Add value, not pressure
Subject: Re: {original subject}

Hi {FirstName}, one thing that may help: {a useful fact — a price trend, a compliance change for their market, a best-selling spec in their country}. Happy to send {offer: spec sheet / price range / photos} if useful.

{Signature + opt-out}

## Email 3 · Day 7 · Proof
Subject: {Similar customer type} in {nearby market}

Hi {FirstName}, we ship {product} to {type of customer the user really serves} in {region}. {One result: reorder rate, lead time, defect rate — only if the user confirmed it.} Worth a 10-minute call next week?

{Signature + opt-out}

## Email 4 · Day 14 · The polite close
Subject: Close the file?

Hi {FirstName}, I'll assume the timing isn't right and stop here. If {product} comes up for {next season / next tender}, just reply to this email.

{Signature + opt-out}
