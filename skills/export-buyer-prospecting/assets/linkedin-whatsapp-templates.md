# LinkedIn and WhatsApp templates

Lint each one: `python3 scripts/lint_outreach.py note.txt --channel linkedin` (or `whatsapp`).

## LinkedIn connection note (≤ 200 characters to be safe)
Hi {FirstName}, saw {Company}'s {specific fact}. I work with {country} {buyer type}s on {category}. Would be glad to connect?

## LinkedIn message after they accept (day 1–3)
Thanks for connecting, {FirstName}. Quick one: are you the right person for sourcing {category} at {Company}, or is that someone else on the team?

## LinkedIn value message (day 7)
{FirstName}, noticed {market change / trend relevant to them}. We've been helping {similar buyers} with {outcome}. Worth a short email with details?

## WhatsApp: first message after they shared the number (≤ 60 words)
Hi {FirstName}, this is {Name} from {Company}. Following your email about {topic}: {one useful line}. Is {proposed next step} okay for you?

## WhatsApp nudge (max 3 in a short window, each with something new)
Hi {FirstName}, {new info: sample ready / price valid until / lead time}. Should I {next step}?
